## 3. Market Signal

**Developer Tools is today's most crowded category**, accounting for six of fifteen launches and two of the top three products. The clear macro-trend is the "agent enablement layer": Dedica tests agent-readiness, BotGauge monitors agent trust, and Trigger.dev wires agents into event sources. This suggests the market is moving past building agents and into making them reliable, observable, and integrable — a classic infrastructure maturation curve.

**Innovative angles worth noting:** WebAI by Opera and Hob both push inference to the user's device, extending the privacy-first, local-AI narrative into the browser and desktop. Genie 4's steerable living worlds represent a step beyond static generation toward interactive generative environments. Zro's "abliterated" security models carve out a niche for uncensored open weights in professional security work.

**Open-source vs. closed-source:** Roughly a third of launches (Trigger.dev, Hob, licenseal, OneDeploy) explicitly flag open source, and several others (OpenLLM, LFM3, Zro) lean on open-weight or portability themes. The momentum is clearly toward self-hosting, no per-seat pricing, and vendor-neutral tokens — a direct response to SaaS fatigue and lock-in concerns.
