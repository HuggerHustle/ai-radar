## 3. Market Signal

**Agent plumbing is the new gold rush.** Six of today's fifteen launches — Render Sandboxes, OpenMail, Orphex MCP, Zero Wire, QualityLayer, and fileForensics — are infrastructure for agents rather than agents themselves. This mirrors a classic platform shift: once everyone can build an agent, the durable businesses sell them shovels, identities, and security. Developer Tools is the most crowded category, but Creative & Content claims the top two vote-getters, suggesting builder attention and audience enthusiasm are diverging.

**The most novel angle is trust.** fileForensics (126 votes, 91 comments — a striking 0.72 comment-to-vote ratio) and Zero Wire treat AI as a potential liability to be verified, not just a capability to be unleashed. That's a genuinely new framing on Product Hunt, where "AI does more" usually wins.

**Open source remains a minority play** — only Deployed Yet? explicitly flags it — but closed-source dominates the top of the charts, and notably, established players (Render, Cloudflare, OpusClip) are now launching agent-era features alongside indie newcomers. The vibe-coding monetization race (Floot) is the one to watch next.
